Aura E-Commerce

Aura is a comprehensive e-commerce platform featuring a robust administrative dashboard for tracking sales, customers, and products, alongside a fully-featured customer-facing application.

Administrative Features:

  • Advanced dashboard for tracking daily sales, revenue, and overall business metrics.
  • Inventory management system for updating product listings, pricing, and stock levels.
  • Order management interface for tracking fulfillment and processing status.
  • Tools to manage subscriptions and detailed customer profiles.

Customer Features:

  • Seamless user registration and secure profile management.
  • Detailed order history and status tracking.
  • Wishlist functionality for saving desired products for later.
  • Intuitive shopping experience designed for maximum conversion.
